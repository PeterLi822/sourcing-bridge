How to read the result

  • Total landed is everything you pay to get the goods to your door (or your customer's door): product cost × quantity, plus freight, plus duties and taxes.
  • Per unit is the number to compare against your planned retail price. A common rule of thumb is retail ≥ 3× landed cost for paid ads to work — treat that as a starting point, not a guarantee.
  • Freight is often the biggest surprise: a $0.25 product can carry $4.74 in shipping. That is exactly why we publish real quotes next to every product.
