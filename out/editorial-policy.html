Editorial Policy

Last reviewed 2026-10-05

Who writes this

Content on SourcingBridge is published under the SourcingBridge organization byline rather than an individual name. We don't invent author credentials, client stories, or case studies — everything on the site is either directly verifiable or clearly labeled as guidance.

How we choose sources

We prioritize, in order:

  1. Platform APIs we query directly (CJ Dropshipping API v2) — shown with the exact check date
  2. Official platform documentation and help centers (CJ Dropshipping, 1688/Alibaba)
  3. Government trade and customs sources for duty/tax guidance (e.g., CBP, CBSA)

We avoid citing general commercial blog posts as the sole evidence for a specific number. Where we can't verify a figure, we say so explicitly, soften the claim, or remove it rather than presenting an unverified number as fact.

How we handle prices specifically

Product prices and freight quotes are the most time-sensitive facts we publish. Our policy: a price figure only appears on the site with the date it was checked, shown on the same page. We never present a stale price as current — see methodology for the refresh process.

Updates and corrections

Product data and articles are reviewed periodically, and the "Last reviewed" date on each page reflects an actual content check, not a build timestamp. If you spot an error, use Contact — we review reports and correct verified errors; error reports and data corrections are prioritized over new content.

AI-assisted content

Some drafting on this site is assisted by AI tools, but every published page is reviewed against the sources cited on it before publishing — an AI draft is a starting point, not a final source of truth.

Independence from suppliers

We do not accept payment from any supplier or platform to change our data, to feature a specific product, or to alter a writeup. Our only planned revenue is display advertising — see our affiliate disclosure.
