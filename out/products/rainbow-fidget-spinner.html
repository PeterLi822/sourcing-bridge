Watch outs

Do not make medical claims. The product name references ADHD — your marketing must never promise focus improvement, anxiety relief, or any therapeutic effect; keep it to fidget toy and stress relief as general descriptors, and check ad-platform policies on health-adjacent language. Small metal parts mean choking-hazard awareness for younger buyers — position for teens and adults. Also verify spin quality on the sample: at $1.33, bearing quality is the gamble, and a spinner that barely spins is a one-star product no matter how pretty the rainbow finish is.
